# 🌱 GreenPulse AI

## AI-Powered Universal Hybrid Energy Orchestration Platform

GreenPulse AI is an AI-powered hybrid energy management platform designed to improve power reliability by combining multiple renewable and supplementary energy sources into one intelligent ecosystem.

Instead of depending only on Solar, Wind, and Battery storage, GreenPulse AI explores energy harvesting from renewable sources, human activities, transportation systems, and industrial environments.

## 💡 Core Idea

When Solar or Wind energy is low and electricity demand is high, battery SOC can decrease rapidly.

GreenPulse AI aims to collect multiple small energy contributions from sources such as:

- ☀️ Solar Energy
- 🌬️ Wind Energy
- 🌊 Hydro Energy
- ♻️ Biogas Energy
- 👣 Human Footsteps and Walking
- 🔥 Body Heat
- ⌨️ Finger Movement and Typing
- 🚆 Railway and Transportation Vibrations
- 🏭 Industrial Machine Vibrations
- ♨️ Industrial Waste Heat

These energy inputs are processed through suitable energy harvesting modules and combined using a Universal Energy Harvester Module (UEHM).

## 🤖 Role of AI

GreenPulse AI acts as the brain of the system.

It can monitor:

- Energy generation
- Battery State of Charge (SOC)
- Energy demand
- Weather conditions
- Energy consumption
- Availability of supplementary energy sources

Based on this information, AI helps optimize energy storage, charging, discharging, energy utilization, and battery usage.

## 🌍 Example

Human footsteps, railway vibrations, machine vibrations, and other small energy sources may individually produce limited energy.

GreenPulse AI treats them as supplementary micro-energy sources and combines their contributions with larger renewable sources to improve the overall energy ecosystem.

## ⚠️ Important Note

Human-generated energy and other micro-energy sources produce relatively small amounts of electricity. Therefore, GreenPulse AI considers them as supplementary energy sources rather than replacements for large-scale power generation.

The current project focuses on demonstrating the concept of multi-source energy harvesting, storage, monitoring, and AI-based energy management.
